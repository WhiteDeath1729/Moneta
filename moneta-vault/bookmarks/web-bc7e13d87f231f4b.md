Reach out to us here . What is Napkin AI? Napkin AI is a visual communication platform that helps you turn ideas into visuals and presentations. Napkin Visuals turns your text into diagrams, flowcharts, mind maps, infographics, and data charts. Write, paste, or import your content and Napkin generates visuals that make your ideas easier to understand and share. Napkin Slides turns your ideas into complete, ready-to-present decks. It collaborates with you before building anything, so the result actually feels like yours. Is Napkin AI free to use? Yes. Napkin offers a free plan with weekly AI credits to generate visuals and presentations, unlimited editing, unlimited file imports - PPT, DOC, PDF, HTML, and MD - and unlimited PNG and PDF exports. Paid plans (Plus and Pro) unlock more AI credits and features like PPT and SVG export, custom branding, and additional design options. See our pricing page for full plan details. How does Napkin Visuals turn text into visuals? Napkin Visuals analyzes your text and generates relevant visuals based on your content. You don't need to write complex prompts. Simply write, paste, or import your text, then click the Autospark icon to generate your visuals. From there, choose the visual that best fits your message. What types of visuals can I create with Napkin Visuals? Napkin Visuals can turn your text into infographics, diagrams, flowcharts, mind maps, data charts, and more. You can customize your visuals to match your content and brand. Need a full presentation instead? Napkin Slides turns your ideas into a complete, ready-to-present deck. What is the best way to use the visuals I create in Napkin? The visuals you create in Napkin are designed to be used across presentations, documents, reports, blog posts, teaching materials, social media, and more. Export them as PPT, PDF, PNG, or SVG, and drop them straight into PowerPoint, Google Slides, Keynote, Notion, or any other tool you already use. Can I edit the visuals after they are generated? Yes, everything is editable. You can change colors, fonts, layouts, and icons, reshape individual elements, or swap out entire visuals until they match your brand and message. Nothing is locked once a visual or presentation is generated, so you are always in control of the final result. Can I use Napkin on mobile? Yes. You can view your existing work on mobile and create and edit presentations directly from your phone. Visual generation isn't available on mobile yet, so desktop is still the best experience for creating visuals. How many languages does Napkin support? You can create visuals and presentations in 60+ languages using Napkin, regardless of your input language. The Napkin interface is also available in English, French, Spanish, Italian, Korean, and Portuguese. You can switch languages anytime using the language dropdown in the footer. What about team collaboration? Napkin makes it easy for teams to work together. You can invite team members to a shared Teamspace, edit Napkins together in real time, and use built-in commenting and highlighting to share feedback directly on your Visuals and Slides. Try Napkin!
